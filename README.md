Liberian-born technologist, cloud engineer, and founder building

## 👨🏾‍💻 What I Do

By day I serve as **Release Manager at TruStage** via Sogeti/Capgemini —

By conviction I am **Founder & CEO of Areteos LLC** — building

## 🌍 What I'm Building

**[Macapeena](https://macapeena.io)** — Construction transparency SaaS 
for African diaspora investors. Real-time visibility into home construction 
projects across Africa. Every brick. Verified.

**[Lynkea](https://lynkea.ai)** — AI search visibility platform for 
agencies and brands. Track what ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Perplexity 
say about you.

**[Lapa](https://getlapa.app)** — Social platform for the global Liberian 
community. Connecting Liberians with events, businesses, announcements, 
resources, and culture — wherever they are in the world.

**LibraChain** — National Trade Operating System for the Republic of 
Liberia. Built on Microsoft Azure sovereign cloud. Powered by AI. 
Government engagement planned Q4 2026.
