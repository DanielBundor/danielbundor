# S1 — The Question That Stayed With Me

**Survey link:** Not included in Story 1

---

One question has stayed with me throughout my experience working in enterprise technology:

**How well do the people responsible for executing an organization's strategy understand how their work connects to it?**

My experience across cloud engineering, DevOps, platform engineering, release management, and enterprise technology delivery has exposed me to the many moving parts involved in getting technology from an idea to production.

There are strategic priorities, product roadmaps, engineering backlogs, infrastructure, delivery processes, dependencies, and release plans. Different teams own different pieces, and each plays an important role.

But I've often found myself thinking about the connection between those pieces.

Does everyone involved have a clear understanding of how their work contributes to the bigger objective?

Can engineering leaders see how delivery progress, dependencies, and emerging risks affect strategic priorities?

Can organizations trace the journey from a business objective through product decisions and engineering execution to what ultimately reaches production?

These questions have become increasingly important to me.

They have also shaped the direction of an enterprise software concept I have been exploring.

I don't want to assume that what I've observed in my professional experience applies universally. And I certainly don't want to build around assumptions without first understanding how other organizations operate.

That's why I'm beginning a structured research effort to hear directly from the people involved in enterprise strategy, product development, engineering, and technology delivery.

**I believe the connection between strategy and execution deserves a closer look. The people doing the work should help shape that understanding.**
